# Flagline privacy policy

Flagline is a Safari and Chrome extension for x.com. It shows account location flags and can hide posts from countries you choose.

## Data we collect

We collect **nothing**. There is no Flagline account, no analytics SDK, and no Flagline server in this version.

## What stays on your device

- Location flags use X’s About Account data in your logged-in Safari/Chrome session.
- Country hide lists, location cache, and focus settings are stored in the browser extension’s local storage on your device.
- You can clear that data from the extension popup.

## What we do not do

- We do not upload usernames, locations, or posts to a Flagline backend.
- We do not sell data.
- We do not track you across sites. Host access is limited to x.com and twitter.com.
